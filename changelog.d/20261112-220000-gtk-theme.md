**GTK and Qt programs match the desktop**: GTK 3 and GTK 4 programs get the desktop's look, in light and dark. Kernel is flat: plates of one colour with the blue accent, header bars like the window caption, flat tabs and square corners. Aero gives them Windows 7 buttons, pale glass header bars and property-sheet tabs. Both show the shell's scroll bar, always visible, with a grip that turns blue under the pointer. Changing the look or switching light and dark changes the theme of open programs too. Qt programs take their colours from the same theme. On NixOS the session installs the themes and starts in Kernel-dark; `services.shojiwm-desktop.gtkTheme` and `services.shojiwm-desktop.qt` set the names or turn them off.
