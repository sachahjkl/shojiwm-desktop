A complete desktop for ShojiWM

shojiwm-desktop turns the ShojiWM Wayland compositor into a full desktop: window frames, window groups, a taskbar, a Start menu, a notification area, a Control Panel, a lock screen and a log-on screen. It comes in two themes, Kernel (the default) and Aero, and you change between them while the session runs.

What you get

Window frames and Aero Snap

The compositor draws every window's frame: a caption with its title and the minimise, maximise and close buttons. In Aero, the caption is blurred glass; in Kernel, a steel-blue plate.

  • Every window floats. Drag one against an edge, or press Super+arrow, to snap it.
  • Alt+Tab shows live thumbnails of the windows.
  • Aero Peek: rest on the right end of the taskbar to see the desktop through the windows.
  • Super+Shift+wheel zooms the screen around the pointer.

Window groups

Drop a window's caption on another window's caption. The window becomes a tab in that window's frame.

  • While you drag, the window turns see-through and a ghost tab shows where it will go.
  • Click a tab to show its window. Drag a tab along the strip to move it, or out of the strip to take the window out.
  • Super+Ctrl+Left or Right changes tab. Super+Ctrl+Down takes the shown tab out.
  • The taskbar still lists every window. Choosing one there shows its tab.

Taskbar, Start menu and launcher

  • The taskbar combines the windows of one program behind one button, keeps pinned programs, and shows thumbnails, jump lists and download progress.
  • The Start menu holds pinned and frequent programs, All Programs, your folders, search, and the power actions.
  • Super+Shift+D opens the launcher. It matches letters in order (fx finds Firefox) and highlights the matched letters.
  • Super+R opens the Run dialog with its history.

Notification area

  • Volume, with the output devices and the media keys.
  • Network (NetworkManager) and Bluetooth (BlueZ) panels.
  • Notifications kept across sessions, with Do not disturb.
  • Action Center: failed services and full disks.
  • The clock, with a calendar and up to two more time zones.
  • Tray items with their own menus; hidden icons behind the arrow.

Desktop and gadgets

  • Desktop icons: the Recycle Bin, Computer and your desktop folder, on any screen, by hand or on a grid.
  • The desktop menu: view, sort, new folder or text document, a terminal there, Personalize.
  • Two gadgets: a CPU and memory meter, and an analog clock.
  • Sticky notes, kept between sessions.
  • A background slide show, and the Mystify screen saver.

Control Panel

Every change applies at once and is kept in ~/.config/shojiwm/settings.json.

  • Appearance and Personalization: the theme, light or dark, the glass, the background.
  • The lock screen, the account picture, the taskbar and the notification icons.
  • Screen resolution, devices and printers, sound.
  • Default programs, startup programs, date and time, ease of access, system.

Lock and log-on screens

  • The lock screen checks your password with PAM. Super+L, the Start menu, loginctl lock-session, sleep and a delay of inactivity lock the session.
  • The same screen is a log-on screen for greetd: shojiwm-shell greeter, set up on NixOS by one module.
  • If the log-on screen fails, greetd's text log-on starts the default session.

Two themes

Choose the theme in Control Panel › Appearance and Personalization. The change applies at once, window frames included.

Aero

  • Windows 7's glass, measured pixel by pixel from reference screenshots.
  • A blurred caption with a sheen, and a glass colour from a palette of fifteen.
  • The round orb, the taskbar at the bottom.
  • Light or dark, and a variant with square corners.

Kernel

  • A deep blue-black desk under a dotted grid.
  • Steel-blue caption plates with a line of light and darkened edges, a red close button, vector icons.
  • Titles in JetBrains Mono. A thin bar at the top of the screen with a launcher plate.
  • Section titles in monospaced spaced capitals after an accent bar; each Control Panel category in its own colour.

Install

shojiwm-desktop has three parts: the compositor configuration (TypeScript, in ~/.config/shojiwm), the shell shojiwm-shell (Rust), and an optional GlobalShortcuts portal. The flake provides each one.

  1. Install ShojiWM from the integration branch of sachahjkl/ShojiWM.
  2. Put shojiwm-shell in your PATH: packages.<system>.shell.
  3. Install the configuration: 
    nix run github:sachahjkl/shojiwm-desktop#install
  4. Start the ShojiWM session, or press Super+Shift+R in a running one.

The documentation covers NixOS with the log-on screen module, systems without NixOS, and the requirements.
